Behind the Wall

The world of The Drowned Concordat: the sea that took it, the wall that holds it back, and the people who decide what everyone inside gets to eat.

01 · The Drowned World

The sea came, and stayed

The oceans rose and most of the old coastlines went under. In the emergency years — more than a century ago now — what remained of the Netherlands made a choice: wall itself in, pump itself dry, and survive.

It worked. The generations born since have never known anything else. Outside the wall are the sea and the wilds. Inside is everyone who made it.

02 · The Bulwark

It is not a wall

The engineers will tell you that, patiently, every time. The Bulwark is a system — sea defences, gates, locks and pumps, watched and maintained around the clock, every seal accounted for.

Everyone calls it the wall anyway. And over time the name has come to mean everything it protects: the land, the state, the people. To live in the Bulwark is to live because of it.

03 · The Delta

A city built downwards and up

Behind the wall, the Delta packs a population into every level it can find. Towers climb above the old flood line; transit runs through and between them; mag-lev freight carries the ration from the plants to the people.

At the very bottom is the Bilge — the lowest level, where the water never quite leaves and the people who live there know it.

04 · What the Delta Eats

Gel, foam or lump

Nothing the Delta eats grows in soil. Its food is gel — synthesised at industrial scale, nutritionally complete, and dispensed in two forms: foam and lump. Ration bars fill the gaps.

The chemistry is run by Axiom Chemical Synthesis, whose plants keep the Bulwark fed. Every calorie is counted and every gram is accounted for — which is exactly what you would want, if you had never asked who does the counting.

05 · Real Food

A rumour, for most

Real food — soil-grown, animal-derived — still exists. A very few people eat it, and they are mostly the richest people in the Bulwark. To everyone else it is closer to a story: nobody they know has ever tasted it.

What there is of it is licensed by the Verdant Exchange, a distribution authority older than the Council itself.

06 · Keeping Order

The Council and the Corps

The Council — formally the Unified Planetary Council — is the elected body that governs the Bulwark and rations everyone inside it, one contract at a time.

Order is kept by the Directorate and the Corps. Their Enforcers are trained and equipped to manage crowds and keep the peace.

Glossary

Words you'll hear behind the wall

The Bulwark
The sea defences that keep the drowned Netherlands dry — and, by extension, the sealed state that lives behind them.
The Delta
The dense, many-levelled city behind the wall.
The Bilge
The Delta's lowest, flooded level.
Gel
The synthesised food that feeds the Delta, dispensed as foam or lump.
Ration bar
The ration in portable form.
ACS
Axiom Chemical Synthesis — the company whose plants make the gel.
Real food
Anything soil-grown or animal-derived. Rare, licensed, and mostly for the rich.
The Verdant Exchange
The authority that licenses real food. Older than the Council.
The Council
The Unified Planetary Council, which governs and rations the Bulwark.
Enforcers
The Corps' officers on the street: crowd control, not combat.
